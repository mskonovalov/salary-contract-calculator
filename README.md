# Salary to contract calculator

Compare an Australian employee salary with a contract day rate, including super and estimated GST.

[Open the calculator](https://mskonovalov.github.io/salary-contract-calculator/)

The defaults assume 220 billable days a year and 12% super. You can adjust these and add estimated business costs. Salary mode estimates GST from the salary entered; actual GST obligations depend on business turnover and circumstances. Results are before income tax and are estimates, not personal financial advice.

The calculator runs entirely in the browser. To run it locally, open `index.html` in a browser.
