How this estimate works

The calculation matches annual gross salary plus employee super and business costs, spread across your billable days. It assumes the same super percentage on the employee salary and, when paid by the client, the whole cash rate.

  • Super and business costs are before income tax. Personal contributions, deductions and tax treatment depend on your circumstances.
  • The estimate does not account for income tax, super contribution caps, bonuses, other benefits or employment protections.
  • In self-funded mode, the super amount is a suggested allocation, not an additional client payment.

Sources: ATO GST registration · ATO super for contractors · Fair Work entitlements
